# Chapter 6 — Governance & contracts

The governance model that sets the parameters earlier chapters consume, and the cross-contract interaction map that consolidates the on-chain surface. An admin key governs the initial network; a bootstrap multisig handles the first 6–12 months post-launch; a capacity-weighted Governor (`CapacityBond.capacityAt × age_ramp`) with a Timelock and safety bounds governs production once the operator-count and capacity transition thresholds are met.
