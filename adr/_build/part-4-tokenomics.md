# Chapter 4 — Tokenomics & incentives

The economic model that ties the protocol together: a fixed-supply TOKEN, the FeeRouter split (an operator rebate and a treasury leg), the `CapacityBond` lock-to-capacity curve (`bond = k × Mbps^α`) with no ongoing service emission, and the App Incentives demand-side bucket (19%). Per-byte FeeRouter settlement and served-bytes voting weight are the wash-trading defense. The launch contract surface is forward-compatible, so future economic-layer products land as additive contracts without changing existing ones.
