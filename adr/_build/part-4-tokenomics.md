# Chapter 4 — Tokenomics & incentives

The economic model that ties the protocol together: a fixed-supply TOKEN, the FeeRouter four-bucket split (60/25/10/5), the `CapacityBond` lock-to-capacity curve (`bond = k × Mbps^α`), the 20% Operator Service Emissions bucket, and a POL-heavy (19%) Balancer V3 protocol-owned-liquidity strategy. Capacity-shortfall slashing is the wash-trading defense. The launch contract surface is forward-compatible, so future economic-layer products land as additive contracts without changing existing ones.
