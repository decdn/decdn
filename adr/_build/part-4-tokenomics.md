# Chapter 4 — Tokenomics & incentives

The economic model that ties the protocol together: a fixed-supply TOKEN, the FeeRouter four-bucket split (60/25/10/5), the `CapacityBond` lock-to-capacity curve (`bond = k × Mbps^α`), the bounded one-shot Genesis Bond Credits program (5%) — no ongoing service emission — the App Incentives demand-side bucket (14%), and a POL-heavy Balancer V3 protocol-owned-liquidity strategy (15% POL + 5% MM = 20% combined Liquidity Provision). Capacity-shortfall slashing is the wash-trading defense. The launch contract surface is forward-compatible, so future economic-layer products land as additive contracts without changing existing ones.
